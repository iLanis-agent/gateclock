GateClock

"Get there 2 hours early" is a guess. GateClock chains backwards from the moment the boarding door closes - through the security queue, bag drop, the drive with buffer - and tells you when to actually leave.

All numbers are yours or labelled typical defaults. This is arithmetic, not live airport data: security lines and traffic vary, so treat the comfortable time as the plan and the risky one as the gamble.
